# Jameson Web Studio brand guide

Version 1.0 approved direction · 20 July 2026

## Brand idea

The identity keeps the website’s established dark rounded tile, high-energy
green `J.` and compact wordmark. The refined mark is direct and technically
confident without becoming corporate: a clear fit for an independent local web
studio.

All logo lettering is original geometric SVG path artwork. It is not a font and
must not be retyped or recreated. SVG is the master format; PNG files are
derived exports for systems that cannot accept SVG.

## Primary logo

Use `../svg/logo-horizontal-full-colour.svg` for most applications. It combines
the dark tile and acid-green `J.` with the full one-line wordmark. This is the
preferred mark where a width of at least 160 px is available.

## Secondary logos

- `logo-compact-full-colour.svg`: icon plus two-line wordmark for website
  headers, navigation and narrow horizontal spaces.
- `logo-stacked-full-colour.svg`: centred vertical lock-up for covers, square
  panels, signage and presentations.
- `icon-square-full-colour.svg`: symbol-only mark for avatars, app tiles and
  other spaces where the full name is supplied nearby.

Do not invent new arrangements of the icon and wordmark.

## Approved backgrounds

- Paper `#F2F0E9` or white: use the full-colour/on-light version.
- Ink `#171914` or near-black: use the on-dark version. Its paper outline keeps
  the dark tile visible.
- Busy photography: place the logo on a solid paper or ink holding panel.
- One-colour print on a light surface: use the black version.
- One-colour print on a dark surface: use the white version.

Never place the standard ink wordmark on a dark background or the white
wordmark on a light background. Acid green may be used as a field or accent,
but not as small text on paper or white.

## Typography

The logo wordmark is bespoke path artwork and is never typeset.

For supporting brand communication:

- Primary/body: `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`
- Heavy headings:
  `"Arial Black", "Arial Bold", Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`
- Editorial accent: `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`
- Technical details:
  `"SFMono-Regular", Consolas, "Liberation Mono", monospace`

Use strong typographic contrast, short line lengths and sentence case for
friendly clarity. Reserve full uppercase with generous tracking for short
labels.

## Clear space

Define `X` as one quarter of the icon tile width. Keep at least `X` of empty
space on every side of any logo lock-up. No text, border, image edge or other
logo may enter this area.

For the 64-unit icon master, `X = 16` units. The square social masters include
additional crop-safe space and must not be tightly recropped.

## Minimum sizes

| Version               | Digital minimum | Print minimum  |
| --------------------- | --------------- | -------------- |
| Full horizontal       | 160 px wide     | 45 mm wide     |
| Compact horizontal    | 120 px wide     | 32 mm wide     |
| Stacked               | 96 px wide      | 25 mm wide     |
| Square icon           | 24 px           | 8 mm           |
| Purpose-built favicon | 16 px           | Not applicable |

At smaller sizes use the icon or favicon rather than forcing a wordmark to fit.

## Incorrect usage

Do not:

- stretch, squash, skew or rotate the logo;
- change the relationship between the tile, `J.` and wordmark;
- remove the period;
- replace the geometric wordmark with live text;
- recolour individual letters;
- add shadows, glows, gradients, bevels or outlines;
- place the logo on low-contrast or visually busy backgrounds;
- crop inside the required clear space;
- use the acid `J.` without the tile except in an approved campaign treatment;
- treat the social-profile halo as part of the core logo.

## Light and dark versions

The on-light logo uses an ink wordmark and the full-colour tile. The on-dark
logo uses a white wordmark and adds a paper keyline around the dark tile. This
keyline is functional, not decorative, and should remain intact.

The black and white versions are one-colour alternatives. Their tile is an
outline so the `J.` and period stay in the same single ink.

## Email usage

Use `../email/email-signature-logo.png` at 144 px CSS width for broad email
client compatibility. Keep the source file at 576 px for sharp high-density
rendering. Add the sender’s name, role, email and website as selectable HTML
text; do not bake contact details into the logo image. Do not attach the logo as
a large inline file.

For Gmail’s profile image use `../social/gmail-profile.png`. It has a neutral
outer field and a crop-safe circular composition.

## Social usage

- Profile/avatar: `../social/social-profile.png`
- Gmail/Google profile: `../social/gmail-profile.png`
- Link sharing: `../social/open-graph.png`

Keep the SVG files as editable masters. The square profile artwork is safe for
circular crops. The Open Graph image is exactly 1200 × 630 px.

## Document usage

- Proposals and colour PDFs: `../documents/proposal-logo.svg`
- Invoices, photocopies and monochrome documents:
  `../documents/invoice-logo.svg`

Place the horizontal logo at 45–65 mm wide. Use SVG in software that supports
it; otherwise use the matching high-resolution PNG. Do not use the favicon in a
document header.

## Favicon usage

The favicon keeps only the square `J.` symbol and is tuned to remain legible at
16 px. A complete set is supplied in `../favicon/`:

- `favicon.svg` for modern browsers;
- `favicon.ico` with 16, 32 and 48 px entries;
- `favicon-16x16.png` and `favicon-32x32.png`;
- `apple-touch-icon.png` at 180 × 180 px;
- Android icons at 192 × 192 and 512 × 512 px;
- `site.webmanifest`.

Do not substitute a full wordmark at favicon sizes.

## Trademark screening

A preliminary exact-name and visual web search on 20 July 2026 found no obvious
competing `Jameson Web Studio` identity or close visual match in the same
web-design context. The artwork in this pack was drawn specifically for this
project and does not copy a stock or third-party logo.

This is a practical conflict screen, not legal clearance. Single-letter `J`
marks are common, and `Jameson` appears in unrelated established brands. Before
filing or expanding beyond the current local service, run class-specific checks
in the
[UK Intellectual Property Office trade mark search](https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark)
and obtain professional advice if a similar mark appears.
